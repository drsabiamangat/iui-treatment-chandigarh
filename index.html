What is IUI Treatment?

Intrauterine Insemination — commonly called IUI — is one of the simplest and most widely used fertility treatments. During the procedure, a specially prepared sample of healthy sperm is placed directly inside the uterus, timed closely with ovulation.

This shortens the distance the sperm has to travel and removes some of the natural barriers that can make conception harder, giving the egg and sperm a better chance of meeting.

Because it is simple, low-cost, and does not usually need anaesthesia, many couples choose IUI treatment as their first step before exploring more advanced options such as IVF.

Who Can Benefit from IUI

IUI is usually recommended after an initial fertility evaluation, and tends to suit couples with milder fertility challenges.

Unexplained infertility
Mild male-factor infertility
Ovulation irregularities
Cervical mucus-related issues
Mild endometriosis
Couples using donor sperm

How IUI Treatment Works

A typical IUI cycle is straightforward and spread across just a few visits.

Consultation & Fertility Assessment

Dr. Mangat reviews your medical history and runs basic tests to understand what's affecting conception.

Ovulation Tracking

Ultrasounds and, if needed, mild fertility medication help pinpoint your most fertile window.

Sperm Preparation

The sperm sample is "washed" in the lab to concentrate the healthiest, most active sperm.

The Insemination

A thin, soft catheter places the prepared sample into the uterus. It takes only a few minutes and feels similar to a routine pap smear.

Rest & Follow-Up

You can rest briefly at the clinic before resuming normal activities the same day.

Pregnancy Test

About two weeks later, a blood test confirms whether the cycle was successful.
